---

# ROADMAP_CONSTRAINTS

Project Sync Framework:

must:

* preserve human control;

* preserve governance rules;

* preserve document ownership;

* maintain reversible changes.

must_not:

* modify governance documents automatically;

* replace official sources of truth;

* perform irreversible synchronization.

---

# FINAL_PRINCIPLE

Roadmap defines the future direction
of Project Sync Framework.

Каждый этап развития должен
усиливать понимание проекта,
не нарушая архитектурные границы
и правила управления.
