# AUTOPILOT STOP Protection — PAPER Validation Gate

Date: 2026-09-08
Status: ACTIVE / PAPER VALIDATION
Implementation authorization: NONE

## Accepted validation gate before any LIVE discussion

STOP-protection must remain PAPER-only until all of the following are satisfied:

- at least 200 fully completed AUTOPILOT trades have been observed;
- at least 30 calendar days of PAPER observation have elapsed;
- the sample includes materially different market regimes, including calmer and more volatile conditions;
- performance is compared against a counterfactual scenario without STOP protection over the same underlying trading opportunities;
- evaluation includes at minimum maximum drawdown, final profitability, depth/frequency of losing streaks, and time spent under pause/size restrictions;
- no LIVE enablement is discussed solely because the sample count is reached: the evidence must still show that STOP protection is useful rather than merely active;
- any LIVE enablement remains a separate explicit acceptance decision after PAPER evidence exists.

This document defines the minimum evidence gate for review, not automatic approval for LIVE.
